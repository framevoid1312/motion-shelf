## Prompt

```text
here's how to build an interactive robotic bee that can fly, transform, and come apart with claude opus 5.5 + three.js...

the trick is making a tiny robot feel like a real flying machine. switch between flight modes, monitor its sensors, and watch every mechanical component separate in midair.

here's how to set it up:

- step 1: give opus a brief: a 3d robotic bee with flight controls, live telemetry, and teardown mode
- step 2: model the bee in three.js: armored body, transparent wings, antennas, sensors, and mechanical legs
- step 3: animate wing movement and build hover and high-speed flight modes
- step 4: add live telemetry for wingbeat frequency, battery, flight attitude, and leg sensors
- step 5: create camera controls, playback timeline, and interactive system monitoring
- step 6: explode the robot into separate parts: wings, motors, body panels, and internal mechanisms
- step 7: animate full reassembly, restore flight mode, and test every interaction

who does what:

> claude opus 5.5: 3d logic, flight animations, interactions, fixes

> three.js: rendering, wing movement, camera controls

> you: design direction, testing, and visual feedback

one robotic bee, multiple flight modes, and an entire engineering simulator you can explore piece by piece.
```
