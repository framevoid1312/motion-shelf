## Prompt

```text
1. Investigate how a selected tool that I am authorized to inspect identifies buys, sales and transfers.

2. Write a specification with source-file references and missing details. Build an independent implementation and tests.

3. Connect public Solana transactions. Group purchases of the same token by watched wallets within a five-minute window, then show each participant’s later observed sales.

4. Link every finding to its transaction. Show incomplete history and errors separately. Never conclude ‘still holding’ just because no sale was found.”
```
