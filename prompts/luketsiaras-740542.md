## Prompt

```text
Write code that renders a 10-second MP4 of a sailboat gliding under the Golden Gate Bridge at golden hour. Every frame must be generated programmatically.

Make it feel real: obsess over light, water, sail, wake, haze, and sky until every detail holds up to close inspection. Shoot it as one continuous, smoothly orbiting camera move with no cuts. Let the light, pacing, and sound carry a feeling without explaining it.

Use only publicly available open-source tools: no connectors, skills, or private resources. No speech; music and sound design are encouraged.

Review your work before delivering, then report how long it took and estimate the cost from tokens used, showing your math. Don't let the report change how you work.
```
