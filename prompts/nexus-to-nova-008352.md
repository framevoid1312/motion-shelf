## Prompt

```text
I use the song from Thousand Foot Krutch: Untraveled Road. You can ask an LLM to tailor the prompt to your song.

Create a 4:3 anime music video where the music moves the camera.
Use the strongest 30 s of the track (a build, a held breath, an eruption)
and end on the last sung word. No grids, no split screens: every shot
fills the frame. No cut, no camera move, no effect without a beat, a hit
or a lyric behind it.

MUSIC MAP (before choosing any shot)
- Remove the vocals. Find the beats in what's left (the instrumental),
  never in the full mix.
- Fit one exact beat grid per section. The verse and the chorus can be
  shifted by half a beat: give each its own grid.
- On every beat, measure kick, snare and cymbal by frequency band.
  Mark bar downbeats (beat 1), strong beats (1 and 3) and phrase starts.
- Find the build, the held breath (the drum fill before the drop) and the
  drop.
- Transcribe the lyrics with timings. Mark the lines worth illustrating.

STORY & CLIP SELECTION
- Log every clip. Find exact duplicates by checking the files.
- One action = one shot. Never show the same action twice, even from
  another take.
- Keep each clip's moments in their own story order.
- One hero gets the held breath: his eyes igniting in the dark.
- Match images to the strongest lyric lines only:
  "light shines brighter in the dark" = the hero's eyes igniting,
  "when we scream" = the hero roaring,
  "we don't make a sound" = a silent glare in slow motion,
  "feet of solid ground" = the staff striking the earth,
  "we're the ones" = the two heroes side by side,
  "GO!" = the gold beam firing,
  "the untraveled road" = the staff stretching across the lake.
- Last image: the rival waiting at the end of the road. Slow down on his
  face, cut to black on the last word.
```
