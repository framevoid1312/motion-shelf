## Prompt

```text
Build a single self-contained three.js HTML page that renders a 14-second animation (1280x720, 30fps, deterministic: expose window.renderAt(t) so every frame can be captured).

Scene: a floating voxel diorama island in ancient Egypt, desert style, everything already moving on frame 1.

Island:
- floating chunk with layered rock underside, whole top is desert sand with dunes at the edges
- the Nile crosses the front: blue water with foam edges, green banks, palm trees
- warm dusty beige sky, golden sunlight, soft shadows, ambient occlusion

Pyramids:
- left: a finished pyramid in smooth white casing with a golden capstone, small temple and causeway at its base
- center: a pyramid under construction, 15 of 18 stepped courses done, visible block lines, lower courses partly cased in white, small open platform on top with gaps for missing blocks
- a long mud-brick ramp runs from the ground up to the top course

Life (hundreds of tiny voxel workers, ~3 voxels tall, white kilts, blue or white headcloths):
- teams of 12-14 haul stone blocks on wooden sledges with ropes, up the ramp and across the sand toward it; legs swing, bodies lean forward
- each team has a water carrier wetting the sand in front of the sledge and an overseer with a staff
- workers on the top platform, on the faces, in a quarry and on scaffolding around a half-carved sphinx hammer with swinging arms
- a wooden lever on top lifts a block up and down
- an obelisk is slowly raised on ropes by rows of pullers
- sailboats carrying blocks drift along the Nile, a camel caravan walks along the river, mud-brick village nearby

Camera and look:
- wide view of the whole island from far away, slow orbit (~20 degrees) with a gentle push-in
- rounded voxels, ACES tone mapping, soft bloom, light vignette
```
