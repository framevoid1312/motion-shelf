## Prompt

```text
I want you to workshop a refreshed home page that focuses on the use cases for viable edge and highlights the various personas roles and use cases that the viable edge works for. You can use the archetypes that you know to be part of our ICPs. So that would be startups, founders, small marketing teams, etc. I will let you decide. Your job is to expand these into a very, very visually pleasing and alive and creative page with not just a bunch of text, but animation that transforms the worlds or the environment or the scene as the user scrolls down. I want you to present to me three but different treatments of this idea that I can choose among.
```
