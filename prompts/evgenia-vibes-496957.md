## Prompt

```text
Build a single self-contained HTML file (Three.js from a CDN is fine) that plays a 15-second looping Halloween scene on its own, no clicks needed.

Scene: a dark living room at night. An old boxy TV on a low wooden stand, the arm of a sofa in the foreground, a window with moonlight on the side wall. A carved jack-o'-lantern sits on the floor next to the TV, lit by a flickering candle.

Timeline:

0-3s: the room is quiet, only the pumpkin glows.

3-5s: the TV turns on by itself. White static, rolling lines, the screen light flickers across the room.

5-9s: a pale ghost face forms in the static, then the ghost slowly pushes out of the screen into the room.

9-12s: the ghost drifts toward the pumpkin. As it passes, the candle goes out and the room goes almost black.

12-15s: the candle relights, the TV snaps off like an old CRT, the ghost is gone. Then the loop starts again.

Camera: a slow push-in toward the TV with a slight handheld sway.

Light does the work: the TV glow, the candle and the moonlight are the only light sources, with real shadows.
The scene must fit a vertical 9:16 phone screen.

Cozy or creepy? Drop yours below.
```
