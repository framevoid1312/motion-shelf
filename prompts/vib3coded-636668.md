## Prompt

```text
Create a single self-contained HTML file using Three.js that renders an animated voxel-art 3D story scene, 13 seconds long: "Kraken vs Lighthouse". Night, a storm, pouring rain and flashes of lightning. In a raging sea stands a rocky island with grass, a red-and-white striped lighthouse (gallery, lantern, rotating light beam), a keeper's cottage and a wooden pier with two boats.

Story: huge tentacles with suckers burst out of the sea in a spray of water. One grabs a boat at the pier and hurls it across the frame. Another slams down on the pier: planks fly and the second boat capsizes. Then a tentacle coils around the lighthouse in a spiral and squeezes it: debris falls and the lantern flickers. The top of the lighthouse snaps off and falls into the sea with a huge splash, and the light goes out. At the end the kraken's head rises from the water behind the island, with a glowing yellow eye and a slit pupil, as lightning strikes.

Add animated voxel waves, ripples and foam from the splashes, debris and spray particles, soft shadows and a slow cinematic camera orbit. Make it as detailed and polished as possible.
```
