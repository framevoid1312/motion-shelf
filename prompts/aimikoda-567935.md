## Prompt

```text
Create a high-energy 16:9 combat music video edit, precisely matching the full duration of the supplied soundtrack.

Preserve the original fight choreography, character identities and action clarity. Build the edit around controlled time scrambling: different moments of the same fight appear simultaneously, overlap, swap places and chase each other across the frame, while the core combat remains readable.

Show attacks, dodges, counters and clashes as layered fragments from different points in time. Let earlier and later moments briefly coexist in the same shot through offset duplicates, temporal overlays, staggered repeats, reverse bursts, micro-loops, anticipatory flashes and delayed follow-throughs. Make it feel like the fight is being shuffled, folded and reassembled in real time.

Use dynamic temporal transitions: abrupt jumps between near-future and near-past frames, cascading time slices, recursive impact repeats, synchronized freeze bursts, visual snapbacks and full-frame resets. On major hits, let multiple versions of the same action collide together before collapsing back into one unified moment.

Layer refined effects such as RGB split, motion smears, directional ghost trails, monochrome impact flashes, negative frames, speed-line bursts, glitch tearing, temporal ripples and selective frame duplication. Use these as rhythmic accents, not constant noise.

Synchronize every scramble, overlap, reset and temporal collision to the soundtrack’s beats, accents and energy shifts. Build increasingly complex time-layering during climaxes, then punctuate key moments with sudden perfect synchronization.

Make the result feel intense, intelligent, disorienting and stylish, as if time is breaking apart around the fighters while the battle remains powerful and readable.
```
