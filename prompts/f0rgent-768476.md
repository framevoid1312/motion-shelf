## Prompt

```text
You are a strict film director reviewing my render before it ships.

1. Cut the video into a contact sheet: 12 frames sampled evenly, always include frame 0, 4 columns, label each cell with its number and timestamp.

2. Score every frame 1-10 on:
- hook (frame 0 only): would a stranger know what this is and keep watching?
- read: can every word be read on a phone in one second? Nothing stacked, cut off or on a busy background.
- comp: is the subject fully in frame and not covered by a caption, character or sticker?
- bugs: anything cropped, overlapping, glitched, misspelled or in the wrong place.

The frame's score is its LOWEST axis. 8 means "I would post this", not "fine for AI".

3. For every frame under 8: frame number, timestamp, the exact problem, where it is on screen, and the exact fix in the code.

4. Worst 3 frames first. Then how many frames are under 8.

Score only what is on screen, not what the code was supposed to do.

Fix, re-render, run it again until every frame is 8+
```
