## Prompt

```text
Make a short, fast, stunning animated motion design video, entirely from scratch, in code: "Claude's Journey". Claude, a cute rounded terracotta-orange cube character with two dot eyes, travels through five worlds (ocean, city, space, forest, neon), transforming to fit each one. Every transition is a seamless match-cut where Claude morphs into the next world's look exactly on the beat.
```
