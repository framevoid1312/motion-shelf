## Prompt

```text
Use this figured bass as the starting point for a flute sonata movement in the Baroque style. Use two staves for the basso continuo and one for the flute. Respond with only the code block. [LilyPond code]

Compose an allegro to follow this movement.

Compose a largo, the sonata's third movement. The movement after this, the fourth, will be the last.
```
