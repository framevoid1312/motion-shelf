## Prompt

```text
Let's build a V8 engine. A stunning, fully interactive 3D simulation of a 90-degree V8 on an industrial engine test stand, in Three.js/WebGL, running in the browser.  Eight cylinders in two banks of four, cross-plane, with visible pistons, rods, crankshaft, camshafts and valves. Detailed heads, intake, exhaust headers, bolts and pipework. Realistic PBR metals. Real, synchronised animation: pistons driven off the crank, cams turning at half crank speed, and a full four-stroke valve sequence. An RPM slider from 0 to 4 000, plus slow motion. WASD plus mouse walking, orbit, and smooth zoom. Working controls: START / STOP, RPM CONTROL, CUTAWAY, EXPLODED VIEW, ASSEMBLE, PART INSPECTION, RESET CAMERA. Procedural geometry only — no downloaded models. Every control functional, and nothing floating apart.
```
