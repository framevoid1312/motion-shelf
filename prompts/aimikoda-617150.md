## Prompt

```text
Build a glitch title effect in code (Python/ffmpeg). [16:9, 1920x1080, 30fps, 10 s, with sound]

Text: "[WORD]", [white], [font name], dark drop shadow underneath.
Loop (x4): letters flash one at a time in their own slots within the word (2 frames each) →
full word appears with a slight jitter and occasional glitches → disappears by [glitching apart].

Background: dark base with bright, saturated, broken vertical scan lines and downward-dripping streaks;
dark areas stay as black silhouettes. Generate it from the attached images. Mix in striped ring/polygon scenes.
Hard cut every 2–4 frames, never repeating.
On top: horizontal slice shifts, RGB split, light grain. Colors: [red + cyan].

Story (optional): [short summary] — each loop is one chapter; colors, images and the exit effect match it.
Deliver: MP4 + script.
```
