## Prompt

```text
I copied the entire notes document and then pasted it into Claude code (opus 5.5) and said “make this game using the godot engine I installed on my computer.  This game is inspired by 1995’s Jumping Flash on the ps1. Give it a low poly look with retro filters such as dithering and scanlines. I want double jumps, power ups, tongue swinging, boss fights and minor puzzle elements. Keep the music happy.”

I compose my own music but since this experiment is 100% vibe coded, I told Claude to generate the music and sound fx too. 

Claude wrote every line of code and produced every sound, music and texture. Grok made the title screen. I played every build, tested it in my Quest 3, PC, Mac, and iOS and steered the game until it felt right.

By the numbers:

-About 4 weeks from first idea to store-ready (mid-October to November 2026)

-About 22,500 lines of game code in 181 scripts, written by AI

-2,400+ lines of AI-written tools that make the music, sound and voice

-40 stages across 10 worlds, plus 10 boss fights

-About 50 enemy and object scripts

-46 original music tracks, made by code with no samples or loops: drum & bass, lo-fi hip-hop, techno, dub, synthwave, chillstep and more

-190 robot-guide voice lines

-140 sound effects and ambient loops, all generated

-All textures generated by code, with no stock art
```
