## Prompt

```text
Single self-contained HTML file using Three.js from a CDN, with no external
assets. A photorealistic, bright sunny-day scene: a red Swiss mountain train
(two cars, white window band, pantograph on the roof) crossing a curved,
arched stone viaduct high over a green valley. Snowy Alps and a deep blue sky
behind, green meadows with small wooden chalets below, soft clouds. Crisp
daylight, real shadows, atmospheric haze on the far peaks.

The train is the hero: centered and fully visible all the time, with the
camera tracking it smoothly from a three-quarter side view as it follows the
curve. Framing works in portrait and landscape. Keep the top and bottom 15% of
the frame clear.

Timing (real elapsed seconds, not frame count): 0–3s the train enters the
viaduct; 3–12s it crosses the curve with the camera gliding alongside; 12–15s
the camera eases back to show the full viaduct and mountains; after that, the
train keeps rolling in a seamless loop.

No UI, text, HUD, overlays or watermarks. Plays automatically and is visible
from the very first frame. The canvas fills the whole window.
```
