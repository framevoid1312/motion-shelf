## Prompt

```text
Create a 1 minute video programmatically. The video should be coded and rendered using whatever tools you need, you are free to download anything needed. The theme of the video would be - the fast paced era of business in the age of AI, with a twist, sharing AI Superpowers newsletter by Alex Prompter - one AI superpower (workflow/prompt) shared a week (5 minute read). There should be a rapid pace of development felt of epic proportions, leading up to the human viewer that holds the power to wield the AI superpower. 

Slogan: LLMs don't think, you do. 
Link: https://t.co/7EMhnAx65E. 

The visuals should be detailed and loud, the audio should also be programmed in however you see fit.
```
