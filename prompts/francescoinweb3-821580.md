## Prompt

```text
Claude Opus 5.5 doesn’t hand you a video. it builds you a camera, and every part comes from your stack

watch the little guy: every card that lights up throws him a piece, and he bolts it on

the parts list:

> the tripod = input
reference.mp4  spec.md  dashboards  data.json  story.md
the foundation. no number without a source, no motion without a story

> the body = render
Remotion, Remotion agent skills, HyperFrames, GSAP
every frame written as code, nothing generated

> the lens = taste
motion-design skill, motion graphics, Lottie, Motion
what turns “animated” into “animated well”

> the reels = ship
timing.ts diffs  stills check  ffmpeg  embed  final.mp4
one-line fixes instead of re-rolls

20 parts. 4 groups. one camera

and when the last piece clicks in, it doesn’t print a random clip. it projects your actual chart, with the 71% that came from a real query

that’s the difference: a prompt gives you a video once. a studio gives you a camera you can point at anything

all 20 parts with links are in the article
```
