## Prompt

```text
Create a beautiful miniature living landscape contained inside a highly detailed glass box. Make weather physically interactive: grab and move clouds, squeeze them to create rain, drag wind across grass and trees, build storms, cover mountains in snow, create fog, move sunlight, and transition between seasons. The tiny ecosystem should react convincingly in real time. Make every interaction tactile and satisfying, with exceptional water, clouds, vegetation, glass, lighting, particles, and environmental animation.
```
