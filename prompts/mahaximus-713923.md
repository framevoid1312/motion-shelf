## Prompt

```text
Build an interactive Grok Bot workshop as one HTML file.

LOOK
Warm cream paper theme, soft dotted texture, terracotta, sage, slate and honey accents. Serif titles, monospace labels. Calm and easy on the eyes.

TEAMS (tabs)
1. Night shift: Scout, Builder, Reviewer
2. Sales desk: Prospector, Writer, CRM bot
3. Inbox guard: Inbox bot, Research

STAGE
Friendly bot faces around one shared "cloud computer". Tools (Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Notion) connect with dotted lines. A "You" card at the bottom for approvals. A clock that runs the night from 01:00 to 07:30.

BUTTONS
Add bots one by one, connect tools, run the night. Tasks fly between bots as documents, and the morning brief slides in at 7:30. Break it: "Always Allow everything" (a draft goes out unreviewed), "Two bots, one task" (conflicting edits), "Hide an instruction in an email" (the bot ignores it: emails are data, not orders). Each break has a fix button.

UI
Team colour swatches. Live stats: bots running, tasks done, waiting for your yes, hours saved. A thread feed where bots hand off to each other, a settings panel with an Approvals toggle, and a "good to know" tip for every action.
```
