[References]
<Man> corresponds to @Image 2. Use only his appearance: bald round low-poly head, simple dot eyes, short brows, orange t-shirt, seatbelt.
<Woman> corresponds to @Image 1, the passenger on the left. Use only her appearance: shoulder-length wavy brown hair, purple t-shirt, seatbelt.
<Teal Car> corresponds to @Image 1 and @Image 3. Use its body, color and interior.
<Fries Bag> corresponds to @Image 4: a plain brown paper bag with a plain red fries carton full of golden low-poly fries, no printing.
@Image 3 defines the parking-lot location and the exterior shot: the car parked between white lines in front of a beige fast food building with a red awning.
@Image 1 defines only the windshield two-shot framing, the seats and the characters' positions. Do not use its drive-thru menu board or its brick wall: in this video the background through the windows is the parking lot and the beige building from @Image 3.
@Image 2 defines the medium close-up framing of <Man> behind the wheel: his whole head, shoulders, seatbelt and both hands on the steering wheel in frame, with the car roof and windows around him. Do not crop tighter than @Image 2.
@Image 4 defines the close-up framing of <Fries Bag> on <Man>'s lap.
There are exactly two people in the car: <Man> in the driver seat on the right of the windshield shots, <Woman> in the passenger seat on the left. Do not swap their seats.

[Visual Style]
Low-poly faceted 3D toy render exactly like the references: flat soft even daylight, matte materials, saturated warm colors, bright clear blue sky, chunky low-poly trees. Simple stylized animation: small head turns, blinks, brow changes, chewing and hand moves only. No realism, no film grain, no depth-of-field blur. No text, letters, logos, captions or speech bubbles anywhere.

[Shots]
0-3 seconds: Shot 1, the exterior from @Image 3. <Teal Car> sits parked and still. End state: the car is parked, both people visible small through the windshield.
3-5 seconds: Shot 2, hard cut to the windshield two-shot. <Man> holds <Fries Bag> on his lap and opens its top. <Woman>'s hand is already inside the bag. End state: her hand is in the bag, he stares down at it.
5-10 seconds: Shot 3, hard cut to the close-up from @Image 4. <Woman>'s hand pinches fries out of the carton, pulls them out of frame, then comes back for more. End state: her hand is back in the carton.
10-12 seconds: Shot 4, hard cut to the windshield two-shot. <Woman> chews happily with her eyes closed and a big smile, holding fries; her mouth moves as she talks. <Man> stares at her flatly. End state: she is still chewing and smiling.
12-15 seconds: Shot 5, hard cut to the close-up of <Man> from @Image 2, the parking lot behind. Flat deadpan face, mouth closed, eyes slowly narrowing. End state: he looks unimpressed.
15-20 seconds: Shot 6, hard cut to the windshield two-shot. <Man> slowly reaches toward <Fries Bag>. <Woman> snatches the whole bag and hugs it to her chest, turning away from him, her mouth moving as she talks. End state: she holds the bag against her chest, his hand hangs empty in the air.
20-24 seconds: Shot 7, hard cut to the close-up of <Man>. He lowers his empty hand back onto the steering wheel and stares straight ahead, completely deadpan. Mouth closed. End state: both hands on the wheel, staring forward.

[Maintain Consistency]
Keep both characters' faces, outfits, seats and seatbelts, the teal car, <Fries Bag>, the parking lot background and the bright daytime lighting identical in every shot. The camera never moves inside a shot; only hard cuts change the view.

(No music)
