## Prompt

```text
Create a complete, single-file HTML application using Three.js and custom GLSL shaders that renders a dynamic, procedural 3D winter scene of a vintage black steam locomotive speeding through a snow-covered pine forest at golden hour. Do not use any external 3D models or texture files; generate all geometry, materials, and particle systems entirely via code.
1. The Locomotive Geometry & Materials:
Construct a detailed classic steam locomotive positioned on a diagonal railway track, viewed from a dynamic low-angle three-quarter front perspective. Include a cylindrical boiler with metallic bands, a front smokestack, steam domes, a cab at the back, a detailed front snowplow (cowcatcher) with red trim, and a bright glowing warm-white headlight on the nose with a volumetric light cone effect. Build a complex wheel assembly featuring large red spoked driving wheels, smaller leading/trailing wheels, and animated metallic connecting rods and pistons that mechanically rotate and reciprocate in sync with the train's forward speed. Apply PBR materials (MeshStandardMaterial / MeshPhysicalMaterial) using dark weathered matte black steel with subtle roughness variations, metallic sheen, and frost accumulation along the edges.
2. Animations & Particle Systems (Smoke, Steam & Snow):
Create a continuous movement illusion where the train stays centered while the railway tracks (wooden sleepers and steel rails), snow terrain, and pine trees scroll seamlessly backward. Implement three distinct GPU-accelerated particle systems:
Heavy Exhaust Smoke: Dense, billowing white and grey volumetric smoke puffs erupting from the top smokestack, drifting backward and expanding with turbulence.
Piston Steam: Fast, pressurized bursts of white mist shooting sideways from the lower wheel cylinders and undercarriage.
Plowed Snow: Dynamic snow powder and ice chunks spraying outward and upward from the front snowplow as it cuts through deep snowdrifts on the tracks.
3. Environment & Lighting:
Generate an infinite procedural winter landscape with rolling snowbanks along the tracks and dense clusters of snow-laden pine trees in the background. Set up dramatic cinematic lighting: a warm, low-hanging golden-hour sun positioned in the background behind the trees, casting long shadows, god rays (volumetric crepuscular rays), and warm rim lighting across the dark metallic train and billowing smoke, contrasted against cool blue ambient snow shadows.
```
