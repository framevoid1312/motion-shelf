## Prompt

```text
Write a three.js scene and render it as a 14.5 s vertical (9:16) video, 30 fps, seamless loop.

Style: 3D cartoon with flat 2-tone cel shading, thick black ink outlines and a saturated palette. Retro-future city with bulb-topped towers, glass transit tubes and 1950s hover cars with fins. Original characters only.

Story: a getaway robot taxi driver (mint box head, porthole eyes) with a scared white owl passenger is chased by a police saucer. The cop bonks a skyway beam in slow-mo, the taxi takes a tube shortcut, does a barrel roll, then the cop drops in: "nope." → "license, please." → the cop lifts his visor, and he has the same face. Captions: "same model." / "different system prompt." The taxi floors it, and it loops.
```
