## Prompt

```text
Create an animated SVG: an octopus making breakfast, with every one of its eight tentacles busy doing something different.

THE CHARACTER
A big, expressive, original cartoon octopus with a clear personality: sleepy and a little grumpy at the start of the morning, then focused, then briefly panicked, then smug when everything works out. Large readable eyes with heavy eyelids, a visible mood in every moment (yawn, squint, wide-eyed alarm, proud smirk). Cute, not creepy. The body squashes and stretches as it moves, and the tentacles are soft, curvy and move with a natural wave (each one slightly delayed after the one before it).

THE EIGHT TENTACLES (one job each, all active at the same time)
1. Flips a pancake in a pan.
2. Pours coffee into a mug.
3. Holds and reads a newspaper.
4. Squeezes an orange into a glass.
5. Butters a slice of toast.
6. Cracks an egg into a second pan.
7. Sets the table (plates, fork, napkin).
8. Checks a wristwatch or the kitchen clock.
Every tentacle has its own small, clear animation and its own rhythm. They must not all move in sync. The viewer should be able to follow each job separately.

THE COMEDY
One thing goes wrong and gets fixed, so the animation has a beginning, a middle and an end. For example: the pancake flips too high and sticks to the ceiling for a moment, the coffee overflows, or the toast pops out of the toaster in the wrong direction. The octopus reacts with a quick panic, a different tentacle catches the mess at the last second, and it ends with a proud, relaxed smirk. Add small supporting gags of your own (steam shapes, a timer bell, a cat or a small sidekick watching).

THE ANIMATION
Smooth, polished, cartoon-quality motion with squash and stretch, anticipation, overlap and follow-through. Subtle secondary motion everywhere (steam, bubbles, floating dust in the light, a blinking eye, a swaying plant). One seamless loop of about 12 seconds, starting and ending in the same pose so it repeats without a jump.

COLOR AND VIBE
A strong, cohesive, modern color scheme with a clear mood. Choose it yourself and stick to it: a limited palette (about 5 to 7 main colors), soft gradients, warm morning light, gentle shadows, tasteful highlights. It should feel like a cozy lo-fi illustration or a stylish poster, not a clipart sheet.

THE BACKGROUND
A rich, detailed, atmospheric kitchen with depth: layers, props, light coming through a window, things on shelves, plants, a view outside. Choose the setting and the mood yourself, and make it beautiful enough to look at on its own. It must support the character and never compete with it.

REQUIREMENTS
- Everything is SVG (with CSS and/or SMIL animation, or a small amount of JavaScript driving SVG) inside index.html in your folder.
- Open it directly from the file, with no local server and no external assets or libraries.
- It starts playing automatically and loops forever.
```
