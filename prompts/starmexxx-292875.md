## Prompt

```text
use jev as the router in front of opus 5.5, gpt-6 astra and grok.

break my request into tasks with clear owners, dependencies and a definition of done.

for each task, have jev score reasoning depth, speed need and failure cost, then assign one model: opus 5.5 for deep reasoning, gpt-6 astra for fast drafting and code, grok for anything that needs live information. never route a task to a model it does not fit just to spread the load.

show me the assignments before anything runs. after i approve, dispatch each task at its chosen effort. keep me on the line for anything irreversible.

at the end, report which model did what, the total cost, and any task jev could not confidently route.
```
