## Prompt

```text
I want to make a crazy video of my own laptop (specs, images and wallpapers attached) using Remotion. You can use any other relevant images or videos you find on the internet.

This is not a typical launch video. It's an edit, like the edits people make of popular characters. Use the attached Life Force audio. Don't show every spec, just the main ones like CPU, GPU, RAM and storage. Make it 60 seconds.

Include all the effects popular in edits: vanishing and coming back, flickering, glitches, all of it. If you can, show the laptop in 3D, appearing and rotating to show every angle, and point out the specs while it rotates. Also show what it can do: gaming, coding, training ML models, video editing, Blender.

For the ROG logo, use the second wallpaper (the dark smoky one) in one of the frames and put glowing red eyes on the left and right of the logo, so it looks like a living thing, alive and breathing.

Make a badass edit and really cook. Don't disappoint me. Ask me if you need more info.
```
