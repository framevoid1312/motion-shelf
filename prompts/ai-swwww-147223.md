## Prompt

```text
Draw MIO from image 1 (her character sheet) as a single new illustration in EXACTLY the same art style: clean Japanese TV-anime cel shading, flat base colors, crisp hard-edged shadows, sharp highlights in hair and eyes, clean line art, bright clear colors, the same face construction and eye style. Keep her exact design: medium-length softly wavy chestnut-brown hair with see-through bangs and a small pale-blue ribbon on the left side, amber eyes, white short-sleeve sailor blouse with a navy sailor collar trimmed with two white stripes, red neckerchief, knee-length navy pleated skirt. Only MIO appears. Plain soft pastel-pink background with nothing else, so she can be cut out easily. Wholesome and cute. No text, no logo.

Create a 22-second 4:3 cute character edit (AMV) of MIO, using only the supplied still images and music, with Python and ffmpeg frame by frame. Match the video length to the music exactly.

EDITING:
Analyze the music's beats, bars and sections (intro, verse, build, chorus, outro). Cut, crop, reframe and revisit the images to the beat. Give every cell a slow camera move (push-in, pan) and a small bounce on every beat.

DYNAMIC GRID:
Use a manga-style panel grid that changes every bar: one full-frame close-up, two cells sliding in, a row of four views appearing one per beat, 2x2 and 3x2 grids where the highlighted cell changes on each beat, narrow vertical strips with echo trails, and two diagonal cells that squeeze together before the chorus. Rounded white panel borders.

CUTE EFFECTS:
Pastel gradient backgrounds with soft floating bokeh in every cell, twinkling sparkles that flash on the beat, a halftone-dot pattern spreading across cells during the build, a pink speed-line impact flash and a burst of hearts on the first beat of the chorus, then hearts drifting up at the sides.

CREATIVE DIRECTION:
Make it feel like a sweet, bright anime idol opening. Show MIO's charm: her smile, wink, shy upward glance and happy jump. Build up with the music, explode into the chorus with one full-frame hero moment, and end on her wink fading to white. Keep her face, hair, ribbon and uniform exactly as in the images.

AUDIO:
Use the supplied music, uninterrupted. No text, letters, numbers or watermark.
```
