## Prompt

```text
make a cinematic 2-minute video on the history of space exploration, from the first rockets to humans walking on mars. make it epic and emotional, like a documentary trailer. go all out.

tell it through the big moments: the first rocket, the first satellite beeping in orbit, the first human in space, the moon landing, earthrise, the space station, reusable rockets landing, and finally the first human on mars looking back at a tiny blue earth.

compose the soundtrack yourself, fully synthesized in code. no samples, no audio files, no VST instruments. cut the visuals to the music.

no real logos, flags on patches, or agency insignia anywhere. rockets and spacecraft should feel true to their era without copying any real branded design.

1920x1080, 60fps. no downloaded images or footage, every frame generated in code. render the final video with the music mixed in as an MP4 and save it (give your file location)
```
