## Prompt

```text
The page is the personal site of Elitza Vasileva, co-founder of https://t.co/Q7sbw4K5Nz: an engineer with a computer-graphics background who also loves music, the gym, travel and food. Today it is a flat grid of tiles.

The animation opens on that flat page. Then the page comes apart: the tiles lift off and the content rebuilds itself as a room, a 3D space that feels like this person's world, where the things on the page have become things in the room. Travel photos might end up framed on a wall, a video on a screen, the goals on a board, links as neon signs. Those are only examples: how the room looks, what each tile turns into and how the build happens are your decisions. Use the page's real content (its photos, texts, numbers and colors), so anyone who knows the page recognizes it.

It should look stunning, and it should be fun to watch being built.
```
