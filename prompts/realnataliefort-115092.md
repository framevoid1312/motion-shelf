## Prompt

```text
Rebuild [any device] in code, part by part:
1. Parts list with dimensions first.
2. Each part its own function. Place one at a time.
3. After each, check clearance (>= 0.05 mm), clashes, bends.
4. Plant 3 faults on purpose. Prove the check catches them.
5. Animate moving parts last. No code until I say go.
```
