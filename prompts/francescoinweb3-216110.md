## Prompt

```text
Claude Opus 5.5 and I wrote a research paper about AI video. here's the 30-second version

in the video below: page 1, animated.

abstract
a video is an engine plus a harness. everyone argues about the engine. the harness does most of the work.

the harness has 7 parts:
shot list · router · draft loop · camera moves · checks · budget gate · stitch

> method
1 draft every shot on h3 lite at 768p (1–2 credits/sec)
2 route each keeper by its job: seedance 2.5 for the look, kling 4.0 for motion, omnihuman 2.0 for a host, flux 3 max for sound
3 one paid render per shot. everything else is a decision

> results
one 20s draft pass:
 h3 max recast: 180–280 credits
 h3 lite: 20–40 credits
7x cheaper. same decisions.

> limitations
the engines change every month.
that's the point. the harness doesn't. a new model is just one more row in the router.

> conclusion
stop shopping for a better model. build a better harness.
```
