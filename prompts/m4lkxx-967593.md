## Prompt

```text
1. YouTube thumbnail → "Make a YouTube thumbnail: huge 3-word title, shocked face, one red arrow, max contrast"

2. Instagram carousel → "Make a 5-slide Instagram carousel: hook cover, one tip per slide, same grid on every slide"

3. Event poster → "Make an event poster: giant date, two fonts max, gradient with film grain"

4. Animated logo → "Animate this logo: build it stroke by stroke, then a soft bounce and a shine"

5. Website banner → "Make a website hero banner: one headline, one line of copy, one button, product mockup on the right"

6. Business card → "Make a business card, front and back: name, role, one accent color, flip it"

7. Instagram story → "Make an Instagram story: product photo, countdown sticker, swipe-up at the bottom"

8. Infographic → "Make an infographic from these 4 numbers: animated bars, one takeaway in big type"

9. Meme template → "Make a two-panel meme template with my mascot and editable captions"

10. Article cover → "Make an article cover: 3-line title, one 3D object, label strip at the bottom"

11. Restaurant menu → "Make a one-page restaurant menu: 6 dishes, prices aligned right, elegant serif"

12. Invitation → "Make an invitation: line-art flowers that draw themselves, names in a script font"
```
