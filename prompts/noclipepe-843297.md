## Prompt

```text
Build a standalone interactive web app in the current folder: "Feed 2030", a concept redesign of a social media app like X/Twitter, as if it were designed in 2030. Initialize a git repo here. All code, file names and UI text in English.

CONCEPT
- A three-column social feed: left navigation panel, main feed, right sidebar with trends.
- It should feel alive, premium and fun: every element has smooth micro-animations.
- Do not copy the real X logo or branding. Use your own name and logo. All posts, users and numbers are fictional.

LEFT PANEL (the hero of the design)
- A living pet avatar at the top of the panel. Invent the pet yourself: its species, look and personality.
- The pet reacts to what happens in the app:
  · a like → it gets happy and jumps
  · new notifications → it waves and shows the count above its head
  · a post goes viral → fireworks, the pet gets a crown
  · nothing happens for a while → it falls asleep
- Navigation items with their own hover and click animations, and an active indicator that flows between items.
- A small "today" widget at the bottom: views, follower growth as a mini chart, posting streak.

FEED AND SIDEBAR
- Post cards with images, likes, reposts, replies and views. Cards tilt slightly toward the cursor, the like button bursts into particles.
- A compose box at the top of the feed.
- Trends as an animated, original visualization instead of a plain list.

THEMES
- Invent 4 distinct visual themes yourself (different colors, materials, fonts and mood). The pet changes its look to match each theme.
- Theme switching has a striking transition, not just a color swap.
```
