## Prompt

```text
Build a new Minecraft boss mob as a real Fabric mod, then film a cinematic fight video of Steve vs the boss inside the actual game.

The boss: TNT Titan, a hulking golem about 4 blocks tall built from TNT blocks, with a lit sparking fuse on its head, hot glowing cracks and a glowing core in its chest. It flashes like primed TNT before it blows.

Attacks (all must read clearly on a phone):

- Lobs lit TNT that bounces and explodes, leaving a crater

- Spawns small TNT minions that waddle at you and pop in a chain

- Stomp that sends out a shockwave ring you can jump over

- Charges at you and blows itself up, survives scorched in the crater

Phase 2 at half health: more fuses burst out, it glows white-hot, moves faster.

On death: a huge chain explosion and it drops a "Volatile Core". Give it a boss bar. Make every texture and model yourself.

Video: about 45 s, shaders on, filmed in a badlands quarry.

Open on an innocent TNT pile that blows apart to reveal the boss within 5 s.

Keep the camera close and low with the boss against the sky, and use fiery explosions.

Steve gets knocked back, lands, slides and gets up.

Finish with a final blow, the chain explosion, Steve holding up the core, and a big "TNT TITAN" logo. Add licensed instrumental music.

After the first version, review it frame by frame like a harsh critic and fix everything: explosions, camera, pacing and audio.
```
