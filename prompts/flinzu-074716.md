## Prompt

```text
Reverse engineer [product] from these reference images:
1. Write down the proportions and list every visible part with the frame it appears in.
2. Build it in three.js with each part as a separate mesh.
3. Label every part with what it does and a confidence level. No frame, no claim: anything not visible is marked unknown.
4. Timeline: assemble, move, explode the parts with labels, reassemble. 20 seconds.
5. Use a deterministic render(t), render 30 fps frames and stitch them with ffmpeg.
Show me the parts list with frames before writing any code.
```
