## Prompt

```text
THE BIKE PROMPT. Make it look like the website costs more than the bike.

"Build an interactive 3D showroom for a premium road bike called “VELOCE R9 / AERO-SLR,” running in the browser.

Model a complete bicycle with a dark carbon frame, drop handlebars, thin tires, detailed spokes, saddle, pedals, chain, and drivetrain. Add restrained gold accents and subtle frame branding.

Place it on a glossy studio floor with soft reflections and realistic contact shadows. Transition the background smoothly through teal, green, amber, red, and fuchsia.

Create a cinematic camera sequence that moves from a full side profile into close-ups of the drivetrain, cockpit, fork, and rear hub. Keep transitions smooth and let users orbit and zoom manually.

Add clickable component labels that stay anchored to the correct parts as the camera moves.

Use compact translucent panels for a live telemetry display and a bike configurator. Include working finish presets and material controls for gloss, metallic appearance, and roughness. Clearly label simulated telemetry.

Add camera-view buttons, an auto-rotate toggle, and a bottom timeline for replaying the showcase.

Use precise typography, dark glass panels, and small orange highlights. Keep the bike visible behind the interface.

Deliver a complete working experience.

Make every close-up hold up. People are going to zoom in.
```
