## Prompt

```text
10-second cinematic video, square 1:1, one continuous shot, real-time action.

A large medieval wooden mangonel catapult rolls briskly from left to right along a muddy grassy ridge. Its four wheels rotate visibly, bouncing over uneven ground and throwing up dust. The machine has a sturdy timber chassis, iron fittings, tensioned ropes, and one long pivoting throwing arm ending in an open spoon. A single burning stone rests inside the spoon.

WIDE SIDE TRACKING SHOT. Keep the complete catapult visible throughout its full range of motion: all wheels, chassis, and the spoon at its highest position. The entire machine with its fully raised arm fits within the central 70 percent of the frame. Leave clear space above the raised spoon and ahead of the moving chassis. Maintain a constant camera distance with a subtle tracking arc. No zoom-in, no cuts.

0–3 seconds: the catapult is already rolling from the first frame. Its throwing arm is cocked backward, carrying the burning stone. Wheels rattle, ropes vibrate, grass bends, and dust trails behind.

3–5 seconds: the throwing arm snaps upward through a strong, clearly visible arc. The burning stone physically separates from the spoon and flies forward toward a distant castle. The empty spoon remains attached to the arm. The chassis jolts backward from recoil, and its wheels briefly bounce before settling.

5–8 seconds: the catapult continues rolling while the projectile follows a high ballistic arc across the sky, trailing flame, sparks, and smoke. Keep the catapult as the main subject.

8–10 seconds: the projectile strikes the distant castle wall, producing a brief burst of fire, stone fragments, and spreading dust. The intact catapult remains fully visible in the foreground, its empty arm settling after the throw.

Premium photoreal historical film VFX. Detailed weathered timber, worn iron, coarse ropes, realistic weight and mechanical motion. A spectacular mountain valley surrounds the ridge, with a medieval stone castle on a distant cliff. Golden sunset backlight, dramatic clouds, atmospheric depth, windblown grass, and rich natural colors.

The projectile is thrown by the swinging spoon, with no barrel or muzzle blast. One machine, one throwing arm, one projectile. No text, logos, interface, or split screen.

Sound: heavy wooden wheels rattling over dirt, creaking timber, tightening ropes, a sharp mechanical THWACK at release, a fiery projectile whoosh, and a distant stone impact. Continuous wind underneath. No music or speech.
```
