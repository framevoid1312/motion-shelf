## Prompt

```text
Build a stylised anime waterfall in my Unity URP scene, falling from [your cliff or ledge] into [your water or ground]. If a waterfall already exists there, replace it, don’t stack a new system on top. If there is none, build it from scratch.

1. Mesh: a curved sheet that rolls over the lip, thin at the top and thicker toward the foot. Not a flat board.

2. Shader: unlit, no textures. One noise stretched into tall thin streaks, scrolling down.

3. Colour: 3 flat tones with hard edges: bright teal body, lighter teal, pure white. Bright and saturated, never dark.
4. White goes in three places: tongues at the lip, churn at the foot, thin streaks between.

5. Splashes: hard-edged flat shapes at the foot. Big blobs bursting up, small droplets, rings on the water.

6. Before you start, set 4 fixed cameras and save a still from each. After every pass, save the same 4 stills and compare.

7. Check your own result against the stills. If it reads dark, torn or flat, redo it before you say done. Max 3 passes, then report what is still wrong.
```
