## Prompt

```text
Create a realistic Porsche 911 GT3 RS in Blender using the attached reference images.

Model every part as its own named object:
- body panels and wide fenders
- swan-neck rear wing
- wheels with brake calipers
- headlights, taillights and mirrors
- full interior with seats, steering wheel and dashboard

Match the proportions in the references and use correct materials: glossy paint, carbon fiber, rubber tires and glass.

Rig it so the doors and hood open, the rear wing tilts up, the front wheels steer and all four wheels spin.

Then set up studio lighting with a dark reflective floor and render a 30 second cinematic: a slow orbit around the car, a close-up of the wheel and caliper, and a final shot of the rear wing lifting.
```
