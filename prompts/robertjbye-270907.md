## Prompt

```text
make 20 interactive dynamic motion graphics animation that shows off how incredible you are at motion design. it's pretty much your interactive showreel for notion graphics. go all out. Make it work on mobile and web. For interactions it should use your cursor clicks, cursor position or finger taps and drags during the animations. let the user click through each one like a slideshow, use cool button animations and say it was made by opus 5.5. make it awesome.
```
