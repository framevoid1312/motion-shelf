## Prompt

```text
ACTOR: a featureless smooth charcoal-grey 3D mannequin figure with a human anatomy and natural human joints, no face, no clothes, no hair.
Heavyset build: unusually long, thick, muscular arms, very broad shoulders, a barrel chest and short sturdy legs. It moves like a real strong human with weight and momentum, not like a robot. Every joint is clearly visible at all times.

CAMERA: static locked-off camera on a tripod, no movement, no zoom, no cuts. Camera at waist height, positioned about 50 degrees to the figure's RIGHT side (the hammer side). Full body always in frame from the top of the raised hammer to the soles of the feet, with generous empty space above and below. Sharp, crisp frames, fast shutter, no motion blur.

SET: plain light grey studio backdrop and light grey floor, bright even soft lighting, high contrast between the dark mannequin and the background. A steel anvil on a thin metal stand, waist high, about one arm's length in front of it, not covering its legs. No sparks, no smoke, no fire, no other objects or people.

ACTION:
First second: the figure stands still facing the camera, feet shoulder-width apart, arms held slightly out from the body.
Then it turns to the anvil, takes a wide stable stance with the left foot slightly forward, and forges with a heavy hammer in its right hand while its left hand holds long tongs on the anvil.
Every strike is big, wide and powerful: it lifts the hammer high above its head and back behind its right shoulder with the arm almost fully extended, the torso leans back and twists to the right; then its whole body drives the hammer down in a large arc onto the anvil — knees bend, hips drop, torso bends forward over the anvil — a hard stop on impact, the hammer rebounds slightly, and it rises back up for the next swing.
All strikes are identical, in a steady slow rhythm of about one strike every 1.5 seconds. Feet stay planted, no stepping.

Duration 8 seconds, realistic heavy motion with weight and momentum.
```
