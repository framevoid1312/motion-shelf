## Prompt

```text
Create a single self-contained HTML file using Three.js that renders an animated voxel-art 3D scene: a giant koi (white with red patches, a red cap on its head, golden eyes) swims through a sunset sky above a sea of clouds, carrying a Japanese garden on its back: a tea house with a curved tiled roof and paper lanterns, a pond with lily pads and tiny fish, a red arched bridge, blossoming cherry trees, a stone lantern, bamboo, a gravel path and a red torii gate near its head.

Water overflows the pond, runs down the koi's sides and pours off its fins as waterfalls into the clouds. The tail and fins sway gently, a long flowing butterfly tail ripples, a trail of cherry petals drifts behind it, cranes fly alongside, clouds stream past below and a low sun glows in the distance.

Add soft shadows, warm sunset light and a slow cinematic camera orbit. Make it as detailed and polished as possible.
```
