## Prompt

```text
write a script in egyptian arabic, that i will use to give to google 3.8 flash tts tool, to generate a voice. the tool allows for giving a description to the voice and an expression for every sentence, take that into consideration when writing the script.

attached is the voice over for a script in egyptian arabic, that you will use to generate a motion graphics for adults about "How big is the universe, really?"  Generate the motion graphics video to match the voice over timing perfectly, go all out and impress me. do not generate captions or subtitles. Use minimal background music and lower its volume so it's barely noticeable. The voice over and sound effects are more important than the music.  the voice over is in the style of planet earth and these kind of documentaries, so the video you generate needs to match that style.
```
