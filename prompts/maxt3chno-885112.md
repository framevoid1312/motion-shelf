## Prompt

```text
Using Blender through Blender MCP, make a game-ready 3D model of the hooked machete in the attached image. Work directly in Blender: build, look at the result, compare it with the reference and fix it. Match the reference as closely as you can: shape, proportions, materials and colours.

What it is: a large hooked jungle machete, held in one hand by the player in first person, so it is seen up close.

Details to keep:
- Long curved blade that widens towards the front and ends in a big forward hook, like a beak. The cutting edge runs along the inner curve.
- Rusty, scratched dark steel with a bright hand-sharpened edge, chips and a few jagged notches near the hook.
- Engraved tribal band along the back of the blade, the crossed-blades mark and the scratched tally marks near the guard.
- Round flat steel guard.
- Handle wrapped in dark leather with several raised rings.
- Curved steel hook-shaped pommel at the end of the handle.
- A dark leather lanyard through the pommel with a shark tooth.
```
