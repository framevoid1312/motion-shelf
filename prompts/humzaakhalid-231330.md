## Prompt

```text
Make a premium motion-graphics brand film for [BRAND NAME] ([WEBSITE URL]).

What it's about: [one sentence: what the brand does and for whom]
Length & format: [e.g. 20 s, 1920×1080 (16:9) or 1080×1350 (4:5)], 60 fps
Voiceover: [none / write it for me / these lines: "…"]
Brand look: [colours, fonts, logo file path, or "take it from the website"]
References (optional): [paths or links to videos I like: copy the technique, never the shots]

How to make it:
1. Read house-rule. md and the brand brief, then study the website and references.
2. Use real content from the website (real headlines, images, logo). Never invent any.
3. Write a one-sentence idea and a short storyboard: hook → problem → clarity → proof → brand reveal → URL.
4. Make the music and sound first (calm, smooth whooshes, −14 LUFS), then time every animation to the beat.
5. Build it in HyperFrames: deterministic, with real camera depth and transitions caused by objects in the scene, not pasted on.
6. Check it with contact sheets, a phone-size view and the house-rule scores. Fix anything under 8/10.
7. Render with node render.mjs, send me the MP4, and open the Studio preview.

Keep it clear, calm and premium: one accent colour, big readable type, nothing generic or "AI-looking".
```
