## Prompt

```text
step 1: connect opus 5.5 to blender via mcp, send front, side and back concepts
- step 2: prompt: “build this hero part by part: bob haircut, wide sun hat, puff-sleeve floral dress, white sash with a back bow, white boots”
- step 3: blockout: big head, small body, a bell-shaped skirt
- step 4: layer the outfit: dress > sash and bow > boots > hat
- step 5: rig it with extra bone chains in the skirt and the bow ribbons
- step 6: animate one clip at a time: idle with a blink > turn > walk
- step 7: add follow-through: the skirt flares and the bow lags behind on turns
- step 8: review scene: grey floor, fixed camera, the back-view concept in the corner, fix one thing per prompt
```
