## Prompt

```text
hey let's try something... i want you to make me a game called "Pelican Bike Joust" where I'm a pelican on a bike and I have to joust with other Pelicans on bikes. The main game loop is a best of three joust and my view as the player is an over-the-shoulder view of my pelican. the game play involves choosing a specific bike and lance to start (with a series of different ones that will do different things) and then in the actual joust, i have to pedal my bike with the a/d keys in real time (faster means faster on the bike) and the arrow keys control the joust point. each round ends only when one of the two (me vs computer) of us is knocked off the bike
make the game in 3js and really wow me with the graphics and presentations
set what ever goal you want and don't give me a version to play until you're happy"
```
