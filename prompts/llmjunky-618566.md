## Prompt

```text
Remake this music video as a hand-painted cartoon starring Clawd: https://t.co/UAPD0F5dAA

Use John Heibel's ClaudeAnimationBase (https://t.co/pbVTIfh5tT), the engine and style of his "I'm Upping My P(doom)" video, and read its animation guide first. If you have it, use the music-video-reimagined skill (https://t.co/ALktuIacjm).

- Download the original and study it frame by frame before you plan anything.
- Reimagine the artist and dancers as Clawd characters: unique, recognisable, and consistent in every shot.
- The dance is the star. Replicate the real choreography as closely as you can, big and on the beat.
- Make it your own: a creative, funny, truly surprising spin, not a shot-for-shot copy. You have full creative freedom.
- The whole song plays, and every frame flows into the next.
- Fancy motion graphics and text are welcome, but no lyrics.
- Storyboard first, then build it as a team of subagents, all on Opus 5.5.
- Take as long as you need. Make it exceptional.
```
